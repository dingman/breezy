## Goal state (live predicate)

A running Nautilus node, with no backtest and no catalog, can answer at wall-clock `t`,
for one station: `R(t−L) = max{ rounded_f of that station's climate-day observations whose
measurement instant ≤ t−L and whose bytes were received ≤ t }`, plus
`staleness = t − newest observed instant`; when no observation of the current climate day
has been received, or staleness exceeds the configured bound, the strategy's order is
REFUSED under a reason inside `COUNTED_REFUSAL_REASONS`, never evaluated against a
synthesised or carried-over value. `L` is the M_B lag (5/10/15 min;
`mb_current_rung_edge_study.py:466-508` lags the entry ASK, not R — see open item 3).
