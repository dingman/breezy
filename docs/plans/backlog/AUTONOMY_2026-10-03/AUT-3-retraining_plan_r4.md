### 3.2 `rung_recalibration`: hypothesis, population, competing evidence

- **Hypothesis H-R (not a claim).** On rungs the gate *selects*, the realised win rate of the bought leg differs from the forecast's raw (pre-recalibration) probability. A one-parameter partition map fitted on those outcomes estimates that selection-conditioned miscalibration.
- **Selection population.** c is an estimate **conditional on one population**, recorded in `params.selection_population` (§3.5): filled `entry` takes decided under the **parent's gate** (same EMOS block and same recalibration as the parent, §3.4(d)), from IOC orders that filled. **IOC misses are AMBIGUOUS** (memory `strict-zero-fill-is-unreachable`) and produce no C2 label, so the population excludes every take that did not fill; it also excludes takes that the daily budget or an AMBIGUOUS latch suppressed. c says nothing about rungs or decisions outside that population, and AUT-4 must not read it as a calibration of the full forecast.
- **Mechanism.** The 09-20 terminal finding: market resolution is 1.98× the forecast's (memory `forecast-edge-closed-pmus-rungs`). Selecting where the forecast disagrees most with a better-resolving market concentrates forecast errors in the selected set (winner's curse), the same train/serve population skew as memory `archive-table-train-serve-skew`.
- **Competing evidence.** (1) The traded-rung calibration leg was **under-confident** (memory `bss-headline-is-the-wrong-family`), which predicts c < 0 (sharpen); the estimator is sign-free in a ruling box, and de-risking comes from the envelope (§3.8). (2) A LOSO Platt fit made the mid **worse**. (3) The pinned nightly's latest `bss_vs_m1 = −0.0399`.
- **Evidence class: machinery proven, edge unproven.** A recalibration adds no resolution and cannot reopen the closed edge. Such a candidate is screened by AUT-4 (`OFFLINE_CHALLENGER`, no α). Any nomination is AUT-4/AUT-5's and, under today's σ, its `FORWARD_SHADOW` is `INCONCLUSIVE(window_cap_below_n_min)` by construction and charges no α (ARCH C4 window-cap rule). AUT-3 claims no promotable path.
- **Rejected alternative:** fitting on all C1 decisions ⨝ settlement (refusals included). That is the external-row information `density_table` already uses, not the selected population.

**Seasonal gap (unchanged).** WP0 (vii) measures, on pre-holdout v4.3 rows only, Fit A (`[2025-05-27, 2025-06-30] ∪ [2025-10-02, +F)`) vs Fit B (contiguous) scored on `[2025-10-02 + F, +28 d)` for F ∈ {14, 28, 56}: ΔCRPS, Δ rung Brier (station-day cluster bootstrap, `roi_bound.SEED`), `(a, γ)` shift. Accepted iff the upper 95% bound of ΔCRPS(A − B) at F = 28 is ≤ `SEASONAL_GAP_MAX_DCRPS` (proposed 0.02 °F); else density refits wait for `MIN_FORWARD_DAYS_FOR_DENSITY`. rung_recalibration is unaffected.

**Cadence and per-lineage sequence.** One run a day at 06:00Z. For each non-RETIRED lineage root in the verified fold:
1. **Ordering.** The component whose last `MINTED` record is older goes first; a tie goes to `rung_recalibration`.
2. **Fit.** Each component runs its `Refitter` (§3.4). A Refitter refusal is `NOT_FITTABLE(<reason>)`.
3. **Delta (ARCH C3 `NO_CHANGE`).** `max_abs_delta_p` = max over the pinned `DELTA_GRID` (§3.4(k)) and every rung of |p_cand − p_parent| for p ∈ {`p_hat`, `p_lower`, `p_upper`} on the **enveloped live statistic**. If `max_abs_delta_p < REFIT_MIN_DELTA_P` (AUT-3 ruling, proposed 0.001), the outcome is **`NO_CHANGE(below_delta)`**. A candidate whose sha already exists is necessarily below delta (Δp = 0) and records `NO_CHANGE(below_delta)` with `reason="existing_sha"`; no new C3 is written (ARCH C3 Y2).
4. **Own-outcome effect (rung_recal only; ARCH C3 P3-4, V2).** `own_outcome_max_abs_delta_p` (artefact vs ablation over `DELTA_GRID`) must be > 0, and `own_outcome_gate_decisions_changed` (vs the **parent**, on the envelope probe set, §3.4(h)) must be ≥ `MIN_GATE_DECISIONS_CHANGED_EFFECTIVE` = the verified `autonomy-policy/v1` block's value if filed, else the `pins.py` floor (≥ 1); AUT-3 never assigns it. The Refitter checks both and returns **`NOT_FITTABLE(below_min_gate_decisions)`** or `NOT_FITTABLE(own_outcome_vacuous)`. The C3 writer recomputes both from the candidate bytes and the snapshot and refuses on disagreement (`REFUSED`, a defect, CRITICAL), so the ARCH writer refusal is the defence and the Refitter check is the normal path.
5. **Mint ceiling (ARCH C4, §4.5; ALPHA amendment).** `mints_today` = MINT rows of the lineage dated UTC day D in the verified fold (drill MINT rows included, mirroring the store; INFERRED until ARCH-0's `test_mint_unlimited_by_k_max_but_one_per_day` fixes the day boundary and drill counting) plus C3 records AUT-3 wrote for the lineage on D whose MINT is still pending. The first qualifying candidate with `mints_today < MAX_MINTS_PER_LINEAGE_PER_DAY` is written as C3 and recorded **`MINTED`** with its sha. Every later qualifying candidate that day is recorded **`MINT_REFUSED_CEILING`** (`reason="daily_ceiling"`), with full `data_windows`, and no C3.
6. **Engine refusal after the fact.** If the engine refuses the MINT of a written C3 for the daily ceiling, the next run writes one further `refit_run/v1` record for that `model_class` with `outcome=MINT_REFUSED_CEILING`, `reason="engine_refused:<sha256>"` and `artefact_sha256=null` (ARCH: null unless MINTED). Any other engine refusal of a MINT is an `ERROR` record and a CRITICAL.
7. **Outcomes per record.** Exactly the ARCH set: `MINTED`, `NO_CHANGE(below_delta)`, `NOT_FITTABLE(reason)`, `REFUSED`, `MINT_REFUSED_CEILING`, `ERROR`. A studies-lock timeout writes `ERROR(lock_timeout)` for every record of the run (ARCH: a scheduled run with no record is a `HEALTH` FAIL, so AUT-3 never skips silently).

**What AUT-3 does not do (ALPHA amendment, ARCH C4).** No K_max mint limit per window, no `NO_CHANGE(k_max_reached)`, no `mints_in_window`, no `k_life` read, no `nominal_only` flag, no `K_LIFETIME_EFFECTIVE`. Nominations (≤ 1 per forward window, K_LIFETIME = 4, α per nomination, `k_life` never reset) are AUT-4's and AUT-5's; an unnominated mint spends no α.

**rung_recalibration** (`AR/fq_rung_recal_refitter.py::FqRungRecalibrationRefitter`):

*(a) Label admission and orientation.*
- **Admitted:** `admissible ∧ reconciled ∧ source=live ∧ ¬drill ∧ p_source="c1_decision"` (ARCH C2: "Only `p_source=c1_decision` rows feed AUT-3 own-outcome fits"), `role == "entry"` (exit labels excluded, reason `exit_label`), non-null `p_raw_at_decision` and `decision_id`.
- **Orientation.** ARCH C2 pins `p_raw_at_decision` = C1 `p_hat_raw` for a YES buy, `1 − p_hat_raw` for a NO buy, matching `settled_outcome` = the bought leg's win. The refitter converts each label to the YES-rung raw domain: `leg=yes` → `(p_yes, y_yes) = (p_raw_at_decision, settled_outcome)`; `leg=no` → `(1 − p_raw_at_decision, 1 − settled_outcome)`. `p_at_decision` (post-recalibration) is read only for the orientation cross-check.
- **Assertion `label_orientation_matches_capture`:** `p_yes` must equal the joined C1 `p_hat_raw` within `P_REPRO_ABS_TOL`, and the bought-leg `p_at_decision` must equal C1 `p_hat` (YES) or `1 − p_hat` (NO) within the same tolerance. A mismatch fails the run (`REFUSED`, CRITICAL, no write).

*(b) Fitted statistic: the live statistic.* For label k with per-draw base partitions `π_{k,d}` (d = 0..199 in artefact draw order, rungs in the C1 ladder order), the prediction under map g_c is `p̂_c,k = fmean_d( apply_probability_recalibration(g_c, π_{k,d})[rung_k] )`: map per draw, renormalise per draw, then the exactly rounded mean, exactly as `LEV/quantile_density.py:440` computes `p_point`. `p_lower`/`p_upper` under g_c are the same order statistics (`:437-439`). Every delta in this plan (Δp, gate inputs, off-support, envelope) uses these live statistics, **never** g applied to a mean partition.

*(c) Base partitions: recomputed, verified, snapshotted.* For each admitted label and each probe record the refitter calls `per_draw_rung_probabilities(percentiles, method, draws, ladder)` with: the deciding artefact's EMOS draws **without** its recalibration (the raw base); the forecast `Percentiles` read from the C1 payload `derived/capture_payloads/forecast_input/<forecast_input_sha256>.json` (single-read rule, digest re-verified); the ladder of the `(station, climate_day)` in C1 order. It then recomputes C1 `p_hat_raw` (base) and C1 `(p_hat, p_lower, p_upper)` (base under the deciding artefact's recalibration, enveloped) and requires equality within `P_REPRO_ABS_TOL`.
- **Summation order is fixed.** Renormalisation sums in the C1 ladder order (`CAL:1651-1658`); draws stay in artefact order; the mean is `fmean`; bounds are order statistics. The recompute runs the **same float operations in the same order** as the live provider, so the expected difference is exactly 0.0 and `P_REPRO_ABS_TOL = 1e-12` is a divergence ceiling. `params.max_repro_abs_diff` records the observed maximum.
- A label or probe record that fails is excluded by name (`p_unreproducible`) with a WARN; more than 10% failing is CRITICAL. A missing payload (`capture_gap`, ARCH C1) excludes the record by name (`payload_missing`).
- **Snapshot of per-draw inputs.** Per-draw base partitions of every label and probe record go into segment `label_draw_partitions` (columns `decision_id, draw_index, rung_index, rung_id, p`, float64, sorted by those keys), the forecast `Percentiles` into `label_forecast_percentiles`. Reproduction (§3.7) refits **from `label_draw_partitions`** and separately re-verifies the recompute from `label_forecast_percentiles` plus the content-addressed artefact draws. Size ≈ 200 × ≤ 12 × 8 B ≈ 19 KiB per decision; WP0 (ix) measures it.

*(d) Parent and label binding (ARCH C3 P3-3).*
- **A recalibrated parent is allowed.** ARCH fits on `p_raw_at_decision` "so own-outcome refits continue once a recalibrated champion exists". The candidate's `affine(c, 1)` **replaces** the parent's recalibration and is fitted in the raw domain against the shared EMOS base; it is never composed with the parent's map. r3's `NOT_FITTABLE(parent_recalibrated)`, the `parent_unrecalibrated` assertion and its writer refusal are withdrawn (conflict with ARCH C3; §R4).
- **EMOS binding.** A label is admitted only if its deciding artefact's EMOS block is byte-equal to the parent's (reason `label_from_other_emos`). After a `density_table` promotion, labels from the older EMOS are excluded (a stated limit: their raw p is in a different base).
- **Gate binding.** A label is admitted only if its deciding artefact's recalibration (form and coefficients) equals the **parent's**; otherwise reason `label_from_other_gate`. This keeps the selection population equal to the parent's gate (§3.2) and, unlike r3, keeps own-outcome refits running after a recalibrated champion is promoted, because labels decided under that champion share its gate. Each exclusion is counted in `params.exclusions`.

*(e) Estimator `fit_partition_shift`.*
- **Form:** `ProbabilityRecalibrationSelection(form=AFFINE, affine=(c, 1.0))`, c ∈ [−C_BOX, C_BOX].
- **Objective:** `J(c) = Σ_k w_k·(p̂_c,k − y_yes,k)² / n_eff + (N0/n_eff)·R(c)`. Cluster weights (L-40): each `(station, climate_day)` has total weight 1 split equally across its labels; `n_eff` = the cluster count. `R(c)` = mean over the probe records of `Σ_rungs (p̂_c − p̂_0)²` on the live statistic, where `p̂_0` is the raw base.
- **Solver:** `scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar(J, bounds=(−C_BOX, C_BOX), method="bounded", options={"xatol": 1e-9})`, deterministic given inputs (as at `CAL:840`). The estimator clamps into the box; `clamped` is recorded.
- **Canonical identity.** `|c| < C_IDENTITY_EPS` (= 1e-9 = the solver's `xatol`) is written as `recalibration="none"`; against an unrecalibrated parent that is the parent's own sha, hence `NO_CHANGE(below_delta)` with `reason="existing_sha"`.

*(f) Support checks.* Failing any gives `NOT_FITTABLE(unidentified)`: `n_eff ≥ MIN_NEFF_TO_EMIT` (set by WP0 (x)); `distinct_stations ≥ 2`; inter-quartile range of `p_yes` ≥ `MIN_P_SPREAD` (0.10); ≥ 2 distinct ladder sizes K **or** ≥ 3 distinct rung positions.

*(g) Off-support gate.* S = [min `p_yes`, max `p_yes`] over training labels. `off_support_gate_max_delta` = max over probe records and rungs whose base `p̂_0` lies outside S of the change in the **enveloped gate input** (`p_lower` for YES, `1 − p_upper` for NO) vs the parent. It must be ≤ `MAX_OFF_SUPPORT_DELTA` (0.02), else `NOT_FITTABLE(off_support)`. The writer recomputes it.

*(h) Probe set, gate measurement and X22 (ARCH C3 V2; coordinator ruling).*
- **Probe set** `P` ("the envelope probe set") = every C1 `Take` and `TrySubmit` decided under the parent artefact with `eval_ns < train_end` and climate day ≥ 2026-10-02, **filled or not** (an IOC miss is still a gate decision). Each carries `(side, rung_id, ask_px, ev_net, eval_ns, p_lower, p_upper)` from C1 and its per-draw base partitions (§3.4(c)). Frozen into segment `probe_gate_inputs`.
- **Parent margin recompute.** `margin_k = forecast_margin(hours_to_settlement(eval_ns_k, …), cfg)` (`FQ/margin.py:30,40`), with `cfg` from the lineage root manifest (manifest equality, ARCH §4.2). Sanity: `ev_net_k − margin_k > 0` for every `Take`; a record that fails is excluded as `probe_parent_not_qualifying` (WP0 (xi) clears this on the 10-02.. records; **INFERRED** until then; fallback C1 field `margin`, §8 C-6).
- **Candidate gate input, exactly.** `edge_after_costs` is `model_p − ask − cost` (`src/breezy/strategy/weather_common/risk.py:756-760`), slope 1 in `model_p`. With `g_k = p_lower` (YES) or `1 − p_upper` (NO): `ev_net_cand,k = ev_net_k + (g_cand,k − g_parent,k)`, both **enveloped** (§3.8). This avoids re-deriving the depth-aware cost.
- `own_outcome_gate_decisions_changed` = #{k ∈ P : (ev_net_k − margin_k > 0) ≠ (ev_net_cand,k − margin_k > 0)}, the parent being the reference (ARCH: "differ from the **parent**'s"; r3's "last minted C3 of the same class" reference is withdrawn). Against an unrecalibrated parent only true→false flips are possible; against a recalibrated parent both directions are counted.
- `gate_probe_max_delta` = max over k of |g_cand,k − g_parent,k| (diagnostic, recorded in `params`).
- **X22 passes only if** `artefact_sha256 ≠ ablation_artefact_sha256` **and** `own_outcome_max_abs_delta_p > 0` **and** `own_outcome_gate_decisions_changed ≥ MIN_GATE_DECISIONS_CHANGED_EFFECTIVE` **and** `n_eff ≥ MIN_NEFF_TO_EMIT`. Otherwise `NOT_FITTABLE(own_outcome_vacuous)`, `NOT_FITTABLE(below_min_gate_decisions)` or `NOT_FITTABLE(unidentified)`. None of these is an own-outcome candidate for the live proof. The threshold is functional, **not** a significance bar: it proves own outcomes reached the gate (an identity-after-renormalisation map or a sub-resolution c changes 0 decisions).
- **Ablation.** Defined as the parent's bytes with `recalibration="none"` and `recalibration_affine=None`, serialised by `artefact_json` (not a solver run on an empty label set, which returns c of order `xatol`). With an unrecalibrated parent, `ablation_artefact_sha256 == parent_artefact_sha256` (`test_ablation_sha_equals_parent_sha_when_parent_none`); with a recalibrated parent it is the parent's unrecalibrated base. The writer refuses `ablation == artefact`.

*(i) Time-blocked own-label holdout (advisory).* Fit on labels with `climate_day ≤ D − 1 − OWN_LABEL_HOLDOUT_DAYS` (7) and score the last 7 days' labels: `params.advisory_time_block = {k, n_eff_train, n_eff_test, brier_recal, brier_parent, brier_diff}`. Advisory only; never gates a write or feeds AUT-4.

*(j) Inputs read.* The **estimator** (`fit_partition_shift`) reads exactly `(p_yes, y_yes, cluster_key)` and the per-draw partitions; an AST test asserts no price, fee or slippage field is reachable from it. Only `AR/gate_probe.py` reads `ask_px` and `ev_net`, as execution cost for the functional count (ARCH C3 P3-7; operator ruling 09-29).

*(k) The pinned grid `DELTA_GRID` (ARCH C3 "over a pinned grid").* Every v5_fit_slice station-day (2026-05-04..06-30, before the frozen holdout) for the five PM.us stations, with its NBP `Percentiles` and a canonical ladder (2 °F rungs, 10 rungs centred on `round(q50)`, open tails), **union** the probe set P of the run. The fixed part is frozen once into the content-addressed segment `delta_grid`, whose sha the AUT-3 policy ruling pins (`DELTA_GRID_SHA256`). It is a measuring grid only, never a training input. `max_abs_delta_p` (vs parent) and `own_outcome_max_abs_delta_p` (vs ablation) are both taken over it.

**WP0 (x): reachability simulation (coordinator ruling).** Seeded (`REACH_SIM_SEED`), run inside `systemd-run --user -p MemoryMax=4G -p MemoryHigh=3G -p RuntimeMaxSec=7200` (a transient service is `Type=simple`, so `RuntimeMaxSec` binds), outside 01:00–04:30Z and never overlapping [16:30Z, 17:10Z) including the lock wait, holding the studies lock.
- **Pools (real data only, no invented edge; L-41).** Decision pool: every FQ `Take`/`TrySubmit` from node logs and C1 since 2026-10-01 with its recomputed per-draw partitions, `ask_px`, `ev_net` and margin slack `ev_net − margin`. If the pool has < 40 records, it is augmented by champion-artefact partitions on pre-holdout NBP rows (2026-05-04..06-30) paired with slack values resampled from the real pool, and the report flags `pool_augmented=true`.
- **Grid.** n_eff ∈ {30, 60, 120} station-day clusters; N0 ∈ {10, 25, 50, 100}; true shift c* ∈ {0, −0.02, +0.02}; `REACH_SIM_REPS = 2000` per cell. - **Output:** `P_reach(n_eff, N0, c*)` with Wilson 95% intervals; `docs/evidence/AUT3_WP0_<date>.md` section (x).
- **Selection rule (pre-declared, written into the AUT-3 policy ruling):** `N0` = the largest grid value with `P_reach(60, N0, c*=0) ≥ REACH_TARGET` (0.8); if none qualifies, the smallest grid value. `MIN_NEFF_TO_EMIT` = the smallest n_eff in {30, 60, 120} with `P_reach(n_eff, N0, 0) ≥ REACH_TARGET` under that N0. c* = 0 is the primary case because no edge is expected.

### 3.8 Live loader widening (G11) and the envelope

New `LEV/rung_recalibration.py`:
- `RungRecalibration(form: Literal["none","affine"], shift: float | None)`, frozen, slotted; `affine` means `(intercept=shift, slope=1.0)`.
- `apply(partition)` mirrors `_apply_recalibration_scalar` and `apply_probability_recalibration` exactly (clip, renormalise in the given rung order, assert the sum is 1 within 1e-12).
- `is_emittable(form, affine) -> bool`: the single predicate the writer and loader share (`none ∧ affine is None`, or `affine ∧ affine[1] == 1.0 ∧ isfinite(affine[0]) ∧ C_IDENTITY_EPS ≤ |affine[0]| ≤ C_BOX`).
- `REFIT_EMITTABLE_RECALIBRATION_FORMS: Final = frozenset({"none","affine"})`; `C_BOX`, `C_IDENTITY_EPS` `Final`, pinned to the AUT-3 policy ruling by test.
- `enveloped_bounds(base, recal) -> (recal_p, min(base_lo, recal_lo), max(base_hi, recal_hi))`.

`LEV/quantile_density.py` (bit-identical split): `per_draw_rung_probabilities(percentiles, method, draws, rungs) -> tuple[dict[str, float], ...]` (draw order) and `interval_from_per_draw(per_draw, rungs, *, level=0.95, recalibration=None)`; `rung_probability_interval` becomes their composition and gains `recalibration: RungRecalibration | None = None`. With `None`/`none` the output is bit-identical to today's.

**Why an envelope.** Renormalisation (`CAL:1655-1658`) makes "g(p) ≤ p" vacuous (`0.9p` is the identity after renormalisation), and any non-identity partition map must raise some rung; lowering a rung raises NO-leg confidence (`ev_net_no` uses `1 − p_upper`, `LEV/scoring.py:70-82`). The envelope gives, per rung: YES gate input `p_lower_eff ≤ p_lower_base`; NO gate input `1 − p_upper_eff ≤ 1 − p_upper_base`.

**Scope of the de-risking claim (ARCH C3 P3-3 conformance).** The envelope is always taken against the artefact's own unrecalibrated base. Therefore:
1. **Always:** per (rung, side), a candidate's gate input is never more favourable than the base's.
2. **When the champion is unrecalibrated** (today; and until the KILL, because `promote_enabled=false` under the ARCH C4 window-cap rule and the AUT-7b drill child is byte-identical): base = champion, so per (rung, side) a candidate's eligible set is a subset of the champion's.
3. **When the champion is recalibrated** (possible only after a future PROMOTE): a candidate may re-admit takes the champion's own map removed; `own_outcome_gate_decisions_changed` counts flips in both directions and AUT-4 sees them. r3 avoided this case by refusing to fit (`parent_recalibrated`); ARCH requires refits to continue, so the claim is scoped instead.
It is **not** a path-wise guarantee about orders: (a) **daily budget** — removing an early take leaves budget for a later decision the champion would have skipped (the cap is an operator control; AUT-3 never reads or assigns it); (b) **AMBIGUOUS latch** — removing a take that would have gone AMBIGUOUS can leave the latch closed (memory `first-live-order-was-ambiguous`; `3eb4a108`). AUT-4 must evaluate path-wise. Caps are unaffected: `QTY = 1` (`FQ/decision.py:73`), and the per-order and daily caps bind every path identically.

The envelope is **unconditional code with no flag**. `p_hat` is the recalibrated mean; it is recorded in C1 (with `p_hat_raw`) and scored by AUT-4's Brier, and feeds no gate (`FQ/decision.py:331-349`).

`FQ/calibration_artefact.py`: `_SUPPORTED_RECALIBRATION` becomes `REFIT_EMITTABLE_RECALIBRATION_FORMS`; acceptance uses `is_emittable`; `isotonic` and unknown forms stay refused; `LiveCalibration` gains `recalibration: RungRecalibration`.

`ArtefactBoundsProvider.__init__` gains `recalibration`; `__call__` computes per-draw base partitions once and returns `enveloped_bounds(interval_from_per_draw(base), interval_from_per_draw(base, recalibration))`, and exposes the base `p_point` so AUT-1 can record C1 `p_hat_raw` from the same call. `FQ/composition.py::build_forecast_quantile_ladder_strategies` and `scripts/analysis/nbp_shadow_parity.py` pass `live_calibration.recalibration`. AUT-4 evaluates through the same provider.

**Artefacts that prove score 3:**

4. At least one **MINTED** `rung_recalibration` C3 with `own_outcome_label_set_sha256 ≠ null`, `ablation ≠ artefact`, `own_outcome_max_abs_delta_p > 0`, `own_outcome_gate_decisions_changed ≥` the effective `MIN_GATE_DECISIONS_CHANGED` and `n_eff ≥ MIN_NEFF_TO_EMIT`.

6. The WP9 report `~/.local/share/breezy/evidence/refit/aut3_live_proof_<date>.md`, with `STREAK_RUNS`, `MINTED_COUNT` (informational) and the reachability verdict.

**Claim rule.** Score 3 = `STREAK_RUNS ≥ 7` plus items 2–5. If WP0 (x) declares the own-outcome leg unreachable before the KILL, or WP0 (viii) fails without the C1 fallback, AUT-3 reports **score 2** with the earliest n_eff and date, never 3. The runs are not independent samples.

**Window rule (ARCH §5.3).** A day counts only with ≥ 1 real fill or a production-path canary fill; the window needs ≥ 5 real fills. A `REFUSED`/`ERROR`/missing record on a qualifying day breaks the streak. Canary and drill labels never train.

**Failure-mode drills for (d).** One-shot `systemd-run --user` of the production script with `--drill <kind>` and the shared environment, outside 01:00–04:30Z and never meeting [16:30Z, 17:10Z) with its lock wait. Drill runs never write C3, set `drill=true` in `aut3_refit_day/v1`, write their `refit_run/v1` records under `evidence/refit/drill/` (never counted), and are refused without the flag. Kinds: `stall`, `runtime`, `lock`, `oom`, `repro-mismatch` (a tampered copy of a snapshot), `pin`, `dirty`, `disk`, **`mint-ceiling`** (a fold fixture copy with a MINT row for the lineage dated today; expects `MINT_REFUSED_CEILING` and no C3; replaces r3's `kmax`).
