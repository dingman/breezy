VERDICT: READY-WITH-CHANGES, SCORE: 68/100

The T-WIN design is sound as the headline test. The weakness is the secondary tests, the taxonomy and the verdict rules. As written, these can label the result FIXABLE_DEFECT or VARIANCE wrongly. The "freeze" is also weaker than it claims.

| ID | Sev | Finding | Evidence | Required change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | HIGH | The independent unit is overstated. The 25 fills sit on only 14 station-days over 5 climate days (10-02..10-06). LAX is consecutive-day (10-02..10-06, all five days), so a single heat event spans several days. Collapsing by climate day leaves n=5, and shared forecast bias makes zero wins more likely than a product of independent terms. The plan does not say how P_c is built for a cluster or how T-LOC is collapsed. "Verdict changes → range" is undefined. | Fill store: 14 station-days, 5 days. | S2 | HIGH | T-LOC and T-SPREAD are run on a take-selected sample. Fills require p_lower to exceed the ask plus margin, so errors conditional on a take are adverse selection against the market, not unconditional location bias. A T-LOC rejection then drives "FIXABLE: LOC_ARTEFACT", which overreaches. | Plan §4; decision.py:349-355. | Label the tests "conditional on take". Do not allow a FIXABLE verdict from T-LOC alone. Use the unfilled Takes and the refusals census as a contrast, if available. |
| S3 | HIGH | "LOC_ARTEFACT holds" is undefined in aggregate. The per-fill flag (corrected error larger than raw) fires about half the time by chance. | S4 | HIGH | T-SPREAD's null of Binomial(n, 0.10) assumes a continuous PIT. Outcomes are integer °F with a sd near 2 °F. Mid-PIT is not uniform for a discrete pmf, so the 0.10 tail probability is wrong. | Plan §4 T-SPREAD. | Take the null per station-day from the model's own pmf, as a Poisson-binomial.
