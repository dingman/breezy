**Your specific questions:**
- **P_c = max P_d.** It is a valid lower bound on P(win in cluster) under any dependence, because P(∪A) ≥ max P(A). The "positive dependence" wording is unnecessary. A lower P_c gives a larger P(no win), so the p-value is conservative. Between clusters it still assumes independence, which is mild across LAX, MDW, MIA and SFO.
- **α split 0.02/0.02/0.01.** It is Bonferroni-valid. `holm_correction(p, alpha=0.02)` takes thresholds 0.01 and 0.02, checked at `nbp_calibration.py:1670`. The weakness is power, not validity (R2-3).
- **T-SPREAD discrete-pmf null.** It is correct for the tail probability. Cluster-level T-SPREAD and the attribution still need fixing (R2-4).
- **T-ART.** Valid as a paired sign test, with the tie and power caveats above. Its interpretation as "v5.0 May–June extrapolation" is appropriately modest.
- **Verdict precedence.** It is coherent except for R2-5 and R2-2.
- **"T-WIN expected to reject."** It does not break the design, because the attribution and K-flags carry the value. But the plan should say the rejection is single-event-driven and add the leave-one-out check (R2-2).
