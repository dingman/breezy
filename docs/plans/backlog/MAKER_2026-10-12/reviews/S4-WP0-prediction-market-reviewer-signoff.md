Own 1-lot taker fills also read 0.01/0.00/0.01 (LEDGER). A $0.00 read excludes a taker-like positive maker fee, not a rebate: the sign flip needs a multi-contract own fill.
- M2: that maker cost = theta_taker or = the bps fields. The spec :140-146 table (fee 0.0146..0.0089 at b 0.70-0.85) overstates cost; conservative, but not a measurement (A923 row 10, FD).
- M3: that cancels land in a halt, or at CLI/METAR publication. Row 13 basis is inferred; skill Q9 (:262) is open; a bid resting through a halt re-prices adversely at re-open and the venue GTD expiry (S-6, unverified) is its only backstop.

- M5: that `unsolicitedCancelReason` presence marks a venue cancel (UNDEFINED on 34/34, A923).
- M6: that "daily grammar" means HIGH of a licensed city or that the listing is the 71 set (LST 10-14..10-20: LOW, PHL, rain slugs; step-0 fixture covers 71 only).
- M7: that non-post-only cannot cross (DOC create-order:82-86); taker races cost theta 0.0695 and are counted separately (spec :156).
- M8: that the 24-key set or the sync/async response holds for NEW, PENDING_*, CANCELED (never observed); that a request `goodTillTime` is accepted in the echo's whole-second Z form (echo only, A923).
- M9: that a cancel POST 200 or `canceledOrderIds` means cancelled (no 404 documented; PENDING_CANCEL exists).
