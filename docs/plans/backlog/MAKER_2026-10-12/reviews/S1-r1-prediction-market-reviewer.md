# S1 r1 review — prediction-market-reviewer — 2026-10-12
Verdict: SOUND-WITH-CAVEATS — the YES/NO inversion and the unit-matching idea are right, but the "eligible station-day" definition and the E >= 25 threshold do not measure maker fillability, and two unit-match assumptions (fill tsEvent, print uniqueness) are unguarded.

## Findings (ranked)
1. [MAJOR] §1 item 1, §6 WP1 `classify` — eligibility = coverage + ">= 1 known-aggressor print (BUYER or SELLER)" pooled over the whole station-day ladder. That is nearly vacuous and mostly measures the wrong flow. A resting NO bid at b is a YES ask at 1-b (leg_prices.py:56-75, :39-46). Only a BUYER print at p_yes >= 1-b fills it. The bid is capped b <= 0.80 (spec §3), so the print must be a BUYER at p_yes >= 0.20. The rungs the rule bids on are far-tail rungs (p_YES <= 0.06), whose YES asks sit near 0.02-0.10. A NO bid at 0.80 sits BEHIND every cheaper YES ask, i.e. behind the best NO bid, and fills only when a buyer sweeps YES through >= 0.20 on a tail rung. SELLER prints (NO-takers) never fill us and are the bulk of the flow at tail-rung prices. Failure: E is reached by days with only SELLER prints or only central-rung prints, Stage 0 reports a fill rate from a denominator in which most "eligible" days could never fill a 0.80 bid. Change: census rows must carry per-rung (or at least per-price-bucket) BUYER counts (p_yes < 0.10, 0.10-0.20, >= 0.20) and the count of Depth10 snapshots with a non-empty YES-ask side (the NO-bid book). Define E_maker = days with >= 1 BUYER print at p_yes >= 0.20 on a rung whose YES ask was <= 0.10 at window start. Keep the pooled "any known-aggressor print" count only as a diagnostic. Answers Q2: use the stricter count.
2. [MAJOR] §2 S-1 / §1 "E >= 25" — the threshold is unjustified and is not in the spec. Spec §6.0 gives the Stage 0 kill as fill rate < 0.1 or a §6.9 FAIL; no 25 appears anywhere. It is a bare round number. Three problems: (a) the unit is a station-day, but the 4 stations share a synoptic regime on a date (spec §6.2 makes climate date the cluster unit), so 25 station-days is roughly 7-12 independent dates, not 25; (b) with p~0.1 and 25 units the Wilson interval for 2/25 is about [0.02, 0.25], so the "< 0.1" kill is weakly informative; (c) the right statistic is rung-level maker opportunities (up to 2 per station-day), and neither n nor power is derived. Change: either derive the number (state the true fill rate Stage 0 must separate from 0.1 and the clustered n that gives, say, 80% power) or relabel 25 as an explicitly arbitrary smoke floor, report distinct climate dates beside E, and make the STOP "distinct climate dates >= N_dates" with N_dates stated. Since Stage 0 is exploratory (spec §6.0) a crude floor is acceptable, but it must be called crude.
3. [MAJOR] §3.1 / §7 — prints exist only from 4d6bb059 (2026-09-16); first journal evidence line 09-17 09:00Z. A climate day D's window starts D-1 12:00Z, so every day with D <= 09-17 has zero or partial print coverage by construction, and the spec §6 Stage 0 start of 2026-08-30 is misleading. Effective Pass A is about 09-18..09-25 = 8 days x 4 stations = 32 station-days max, times ~0.91 listing and the coverage gates = roughly 25-29, so E >= 25 on Pass A alone is borderline-unreachable and Q4 is effectively answered "no, Pass B is needed". The plan neither states this nor tags pre-09-17 days with a distinct reason (e.g. `PRINTS_NOT_CAPTURED`); they would be counted under "no known-aggressor print", polluting the eligible-rate used for the S-1 projection ("measured per-day eligible rate"). Change: add a closed reason `pre_trade_capture` (window start before the first print on tape), exclude those days from the rate denominator, and say so in the doc.
4. [MAJOR] §6 WP2 matching — the own-fill timestamp is not a clean execution time. DurableFillRecord.ts_event on a RESOLVER record is the discovery time, an upper bound (client.py:933-987, field comment on `fee_coefficient_at_fill`/resolver); memory records the first live orders as AMBIGUOUS / GET-resolved. A +-5 s window is therefore unsafe for exactly those fills, and plan §7 only flags it "UNVERIFIED". Also `trade_id` distinguishes create (venue tradeId) from resolver (`GET-<id>`) provenance and the plan ignores it. Change: before matching, read `trade_id` / source for each of the 3 pre-freeze fills and report per fill whether tsEvent is a create-path time. Resolver-path fills must use a widened window (state it) and may not be the sole basis of a verdict.
5. [MAJOR] §6 WP2 `classify_trade_quantity_unit` — resolution rule is "every match fits exactly one hypothesis, >= 2 matches, >= 1 NO-leg", but candidate selection is "nearest" and a 1-contract print at the same price/side within 5 s from a DIFFERENT taker also equals 1.0. On an active ladder this coincidence is likely, and it silently confirms CONTRACTS. Only 3 fills exist (2 NO @0.12, 1 YES @0.35; both NO fills share the same price, so they add little independent information). Change: require a UNIQUE candidate (side + price + window) per fill; if more than one candidate exists, the match is ambiguous and does not count. Resolve only if >= 2 unique NO-leg matches agree. Document that the YES-leg match predicts (1, 0.35, 0.35) and cannot separate the USD hypotheses.
6. [MINOR] §6 WP2 hypothesis set — closed at three. Missing: a fractional-contract venue unit (journal prints 10.33 @0.19 and 3.25 @0.47 are non-integer under CONTRACTS and under both USD hypotheses, so integer-ness is no discriminator, as the plan says) and size net of fee or split across makers (Q5). The sum-of-prints option in Q5 is right but must be bounded: sum over prints that share side and price and fall inside the window, and the sum must equal the prediction exactly; a sum is only admissible if no single print matches. Answer Q5: yes, allow a same-price/side sum within the window, flagged as `SUMMED`, never as the sole basis of a verdict.
7. [MINOR] §5.3 / §3.2 — the inversion text is correct (YES BUYER print at p_yes >= 1-b, equivalently a NO-frame SELLER at <= b) and agrees with `_TAKER_SIDES` (parsing.py:283-290) and the parser comment (parsing.py:270-282). Two additions: (i) a fill needs p_yes >= 1-b only if our YES ask is the best remaining ask; prints at p_yes > 1-b imply every cheaper YES ask was swept first, which is exactly the adverse-selection signature spec §6.9 tests, so S1 should record the price bucket (finding 1) so S2 can condition on it; (ii) the plan ignores the print's `maker`/`taker.intent`; the parser deliberately ignores intent, so S1 need not read it, but state that "NO seller closing" vs "YES buyer opening" are indistinguishable by design and irrelevant to fills.
8. [MINOR] §6 WP1 coverage — `coverage_fraction` has no defined denominator. Depth10 events fire on change, so "0.90" of what (minutes with a snapshot within 30 min? fraction of window not inside a gap)? Thresholds are "declared before the run" but cannot be reviewed as written. Also coverage should be computed on snapshots with a non-empty YES-ask side, since spec §6.1 credits no fill from an implied price when the NO bid side is empty. Define the formula in the plan. A reusable raw-column depth streamer already exists (`_stream_depth_rows`, cli_basis_offer_gate_scan.py, used by `build_scan`), which removes the "UNVERIFIED column scan" risk in §7.
9. [MINOR] §3.2 / WP2 F1 — the 25 post-freeze fills (10-01..10-05) are used as a "no extra forfeiture" fallback. If any is a NYC fill, WP1's own `test_nyc_is_refused_even_with_declaration` refuses it. Verify station mix before promising F1; otherwise F1 needs its own declaration scope.

## What the plan gets right
- The inversion in §5.3 is correct (BUYER YES print at p_yes >= 1-b fills a resting NO bid), matches the code, and the S-3 premise test (NO-taker prints SELLER at 1-cost, YES-taker prints BUYER at cost) is a real falsifier.
- Using the NO-leg fills to separate the USD hypotheses is correct: at NO cost 0.12 the predictions are 1, 0.88 and 0.12, while a YES-only match cannot separate (1, 0.35, 0.35).
- It declines to reuse the afternoon-window AUD-09a census (12:00-17:00 LST is disjoint from the maker window) and builds a small sidecar instead of altering the 20-caller ReplaySufficiency schema; it respects the RA-13 B-3 firewall with a dated SEARCH declaration and a tally.
- Unit stays unconverted in the parser; fails closed to `UNRESOLVED`; no network, no Nautilus change, no exec import; thresholds frozen before the run.
