## B26 verdict — ambiguous metric, both readings reported

The plan text ("<3 GB peak") does not say which peak. Reporting both,
never silently choosing:

**Reading A — cgroup `memory.peak` (what the deployed unit's
`MemoryHigh=3G`/`MemoryMax=4G` actually measures and enforces; page cache
counts because cgroup v2 `memory.current` includes reclaimable file pages):**
**FAILS.** Census alone peaks at 11.0 GiB (~3.9x `MemoryMax`, ~9x the 3G
`MemoryHigh` threshold this criterion names); the replay driver alone peaks
at 4.18 GiB (also over both the unit's 3G/4G caps and this criterion's
"<3 GB"). This is consistent with, and explains, today's two production
runs pinning at "3G memory peak": under `MemoryHigh=3G` the kernel forces
continuous reclaim of clean page cache to hold `memory.current` near 3G
rather than letting it climb to its natural size, which is the named
wall-time-inflation mechanism (plan F4, "L-49"). Because reclaim of clean,
read-only Parquet page cache is cheap but constant, this degrades wall
time rather than causing an OOM kill — consistent with the units not
finishing rather than being killed.

**Reading B — process-level anon/RSS (the actual non-reclaimable working
set; page cache excluded because it is reclaimable under pressure and not
what would trigger an OOM in a healthy reclaim path):** **PASSES**, with a
real but narrow margin. Peak RSS across the two stages, run sequentially
(never concurrently, so their peaks do not add), is
max(0.41 GB, 2.49 GB) = 2.49 GB < 3 GB (~17% headroom; the cgroup-sampled
`anon` peak for the replay stage, 2.24 GB, is even lower). Combined wall
827.2 s < 900 s (8% headroom).

**No fix applied.** The task's bounding trigger ("if replay ANON >
~2.5 GB") was not met — replay anon peaked at 2.24 GB (cgroup `anon` stat)
/ 2.49 GB (`time -v` max RSS), both under the ~2.5 GB threshold, so no
`start=`/`end=`/instrument-id narrowing was made to
`_select_capture_instruments`'s catalog queries and no RED/GREEN test or
identity-diff proof was produced this run. This is close enough (83-91% of
the 2.5 GB trigger) that a busier station-day (more instruments, longer
in-window density) could cross it; the "all stations, no ts_init bound"
mechanism above is the concrete lever if a future measurement does.

## Open items for whoever next reviews B26

- Which peak the plan's authors meant is undecided here; resolve through
  the plan's own review process (architect / trading-bot-architect), not
  by this evidence doc picking one.
- If Reading A is the binding one, the fix is not a per-run measurement
  change but a structural one: either raise the unit's `MemoryHigh`/`MemoryMax`
  (accepting the page-cache-driven wall-time risk) or bound the census's own
  catalog reads (out of scope for this task — the census was not modified
  here; AUD-09a/B24/B25 own that surface).
- The replay driver's station-filtering gap in `_select_capture_instruments`
  (loads every station's same-day contracts, not just the requested
  `--station`) is real and separate from any `ts_init` bound; it was not
  fixed here because the ANON threshold was not crossed, but it is the
  first place to look if it is.
